Solutions

Five kinds of system, each aimed at work that is slow, repetitive or easy to get wrong.

Sales and revenue automation

A pipeline that runs itself from first contact to booked call, with a person stepping in only where it counts.

What we build

  • Lead capture from forms, email and ads into one place
  • Automatic enrichment and a scored, explained qualification
  • Follow-up sequences that stop when a lead replies
  • Calendar booking and payment links, with records written back to your CRM

A good fit when leads sit unanswered, or your team retypes the same details into several tools.

AI agents

Software that reads a request, decides what to do and does it in your tools, then shows exactly what it did.

What we build

  • Agents for support triage, scheduling, data entry and reporting
  • Clear limits on what each agent may change
  • A full log of every decision and action
  • Hand-off to a person when confidence is low

A good fit when a task follows the same rules every time but still needs someone to read and judge each case.

Knowledge systems

Search and answers across your own documents, with the source passage shown next to every answer.

What we build

  • Document sync from drives, wikis and email
  • Retrieval tuned to your content, not a generic index
  • Answers that cite the page they came from
  • A list of questions the system could not answer, so you know what to document next

A good fit when people lose time hunting through files, or the same questions reach the same few experts.

Process and CRM integration

Your CRM, billing, email and forms connected, so each fact is entered once and appears everywhere it should.

What we build

  • Two-way sync between CRM, accounting and communication tools
  • Workflows built in n8n, Make or Zapier, chosen to fit your team
  • Error alerts and retry rules for every connection
  • Data clean-up so the first sync starts from correct records

A good fit when your tools each hold part of the picture and someone reconciles them by hand.

Custom web apps and dashboards

Internal tools and customer-facing apps built around how your business works, not around a template.

What we build

  • Dashboards that show the few numbers you check each day
  • Customer portals and internal admin tools
  • Apps built in Next.js with a clean path to add features
  • Hand-over with documentation your next developer can read

A good fit when a spreadsheet has become the system and it is starting to break.
